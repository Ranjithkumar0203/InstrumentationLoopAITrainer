# Engineering scope and review checklist

## Potential assistant functions

- Explain typical instrument loop signal paths.
- Extract and cross-check tags and signal types from approved source documents.
- Draft loop-diagram content and termination schedules for engineer review.
- Identify missing information and document discrepancies.
- Explain AI/AO/DI/DO and common communication interfaces.
- Prepare loop-check checklists from approved procedures.
- Assist with instrument datasheet and cable/JB schedule reviews.

## Never assume

- Instrument range, units, process connection or materials.
- DCS/PLC channel number, cabinet, rack or slot.
- JB number, terminal number, cable number or core allocation.
- Barrier/isolator selection or hazardous-area suitability.
- Fail-open/fail-close action or safety function.
- Grounding, shield termination or cable segregation rules.
- Compliance with a standard or project specification.

These must come from approved project documents and engineering decisions.

## Human review checklist

1. Confirm source documents and revision status.
2. Confirm tag, service, location, range and signal.
3. Trace signal direction and every termination.
4. Verify power, load, channel and protocol compatibility.
5. Verify hazardous-area and functional-safety requirements where applicable.
6. Verify cable, core, segregation, shield and grounding details.
7. Cross-check P&ID, instrument index, I/O list, datasheet, loop drawing and schedules.
8. Record assumptions, open points and source references.
9. Obtain required discipline and project approvals before issue or field use.
